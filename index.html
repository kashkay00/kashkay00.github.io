Heritage & Evolution

The Porsche 911 GT3 RS began as a high-performance version of the 911 GT3, inspired by Porsche's racing cars. Over the years, each generation added more track-focused technology while remaining legal to drive on public roads.

Every GT3 RS generation has pushed the idea of a road-legal race car even further. Porsche has used motorsport lessons to sharpen the chassis, improve braking, and create more aggressive aero packages.

GT3 RS Meaning

GT3 refers to Porsche's road-legal performance cars inspired by the GT racing category. The GT3 name has become associated with naturally aspirated engines, motorsport technology, and track-focused performance.

RS stands for Rennsport, the German word for "racing sport." In Porsche's naming system, RS identifies models with an even stronger focus on performance, lightweight construction, aerodynamics, and track capability.

Together, GT3 RS represents one of Porsche's most track-focused road cars, combining motorsport-inspired engineering with the ability to be driven on public roads.

Track-Born Performance

The GT3 RS has achieved recognition for its impressive track performance and precise handling. Its large rear wing, active aerodynamics, front and rear spoilers, and racing-inspired suspension help create high levels of downforce. The engine produces 518 horsepower and reaches 9,000 RPM, while the seven-speed PDK transmission delivers quick gear changes.

Aerodynamic Design

The design language of the GT3 RS is centered around aerodynamics and function. The wide stance, large rear wing, vented bodywork, and lightweight materials all contribute to performance.
